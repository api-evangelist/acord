ACORD is the global standards-setting body for the insurance industry, publishing the data standards that insurers, reinsurers, brokers, MGAs and software vendors use to exchange policy, claims, party, underwriting, accounting and settlement data: ACORD XML and AL3 for property & casualty, Life & Annuity XML and DTCC EDI, the Global Reinsurance & Large Commercial (GRLC) standards behind EBOT, ECOT, GPM and CRP, and the JSON/YAML Next-Generation Digital Standards (NGDS) aimed at REST APIs and microservices. ACORD operates no public API of its own; standards and schemas are delivered to members under licence, and its commercial subsidiary ACORD Solutions Group runs the ADEPT data exchange platform under separate agreement.

**Areas:** Claims · Data Standards · Insurance · Policy · Property and Casualty · Reinsurance · Standards · Underwriting · XML

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for ACORD: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
