# ACORD

> ACORD is the global standards-setting body for the insurance industry. It publishes the
> data standards — ACORD XML, AL3, Next-Generation Digital Standards (NGDS), and the Global
> Reinsurance & Large Commercial (GRLC) standards — that insurers, reinsurers, brokers,
> MGAs and software vendors use to exchange policy, claims, party, underwriting, accounting
> and settlement data. ACORD does not operate a public API. Its standards and schemas are
> delivered to members under licence, and its commercial subsidiary, ACORD Solutions Group,
> operates the ADEPT data exchange platform under separate agreement.

## Standards

- [ACORD Data Standards](https://www.acord.org/standards-architecture/acord-data-standards): The standards portfolio — P&C XML and AL3, Life & Annuity XML and DTCC EDI, Global Reinsurance & Large Commercial XML/JSON, Multi-Functional, Asia-Pacific, Southern Africa, and Global Common Services (Naming & Design Rules, DRI, AMS SOAP).
- [Next-Generation Digital Standards (NGDS)](https://www.acord.org/standards-architecture/acord-data-standards/next-generation-digital-standards): Granular, transaction-centric JSON/YAML standards for REST APIs, microservices and IoT. Current final release v1-13-0 (May 2026); prior releases v1-12-0 (Nov 2025), v1-11-0 (May 2025). Ships Master Object List, Master Resource Definitions, Master CodeLists, Master Deprecation List, Schemas With Reference and Schemas Without Reference — all member-gated.
- [Getting Started with NGDS](https://www.acord.org/docs/default-source/standards-multi-functional-factsheets/getting-starting-with-ngds.pdf): Public PDF factsheet, no sign-in required.
- [Global Reinsurance & Large Commercial Data Standards](https://www.acord.org/standards-architecture/acord-data-standards/Global_Reinsurance_Data_Standards): GRLC message families — GPM (placing), CRP (contract, risk, pre-accounting), EBOT (accounting), ECOT (claim movement).
- [ACORD Reference Architecture](https://www.acord.org/standards-architecture/reference-architecture): Structural framework aligning the standards to insurance business capabilities.
- [Standards Downloads](https://www.acord.org/standards-architecture/acord-data-standards/standards-downloads): The download index. Files require ACORD membership plus an acord.org account.
- [NGDS Object Model](https://standards.acord.org/#/ngds/objectmodel): Angular single-page application ("Standards Online"). Member sign-in; the host answers 403 to anything but `/`.

## ACORD Solutions Group (commercial subsidiary)

- [ACORD Solutions Group](https://www.acordsolutions.com/): Operates ADEPT, ACORD Conductor, ACORD Converter and ACORD Transcriber.

With a RESTful API architecture", gateway for global reinsurance and specialty markets. **API specifications are not public** — released to ACORD GRLC members and licensed integrators at ASG's discretion.
